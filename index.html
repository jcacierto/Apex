Apex Drone

Project Apex is a personal drone project to enhance my hands-on skills, teach myself the basics of aerospace engineering, and get real-life experience with systems engineering. This site contains documented versions of my drone as it goes through iterations.

Why Apex

“According to all known laws of aviation, there is no way a bee should be able to fly. Its wings are too small to get its fat little body off the ground. The bee, of course, flies anyway because bees don't care what humans think is impossible.”

— BARRY B. BENSON, BEE MOVIE

Build a drone, version by version, and actually understand every system in it.
