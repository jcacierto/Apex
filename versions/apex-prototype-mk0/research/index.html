Flight Controller and Electronic Speed Controller

Flight controllers are essentially the brain of the whole drone, while Electronic speed controllers are the muscles. They act as intermediary power and signal translator between the FC and the motors.

Two of the most common open-source flight controller firmwares on the market are ArduPilot and Betaflight.

ArduPilot

  • full-featured autopilot system designed for deep autonomy and complex missions
  • best for projects with autonomous navigation
  • steep learning curve with a vast number of customizable parameters, greater sensitivity to vibrations, and more demanding calibration needs

Betaflight

  • built for manual FPV and agility
  • user-friendly configurator with intuitive presets, quick PID tuning, and mobile app integration

Based on this comparison, Betaflight seems to be the better option for the prototype.

Many sellers sell FC and ESC as a stack. A common one on the market is a F405 Flight Stack.

Relevant Specs and Dimensions (taken from a SpeedyBee F405 V4 stack):

  • Weight: 34.0 g
  • Mounting 30.5 x 30.5 mm

Propellers

Note: Many sources will refer to drones by their “size”, like 5-inch drone. Keep in mind that this refers to the propeller size, not the overall width of the drone.

Most drone propellers use a standard three-number sizing format:

  • Diameter (1st number): larger props move more air and provide better hover efficiency and stability; smaller props offer faster acceleration and nimble response times
  • Pitch (2nd number): Theoretical distance the prop moves forward in one full rotation
    • Low pitch: smoother flight, lower top speed, better efficiency, and less motor strain
    • High pitch: more aggressive speed and thrust; higher current draw and potential motor/ESC overheating
  • Blade Count (3rd number): number of blades
    • 2 blades - highest efficiency and top speed - good for cruising or long range
    • 3 blades - balanced mix of grip, thrust, and smoothness
    • 4 blades - extra thrust and instant punch; lower efficiency and higher power draw

For tiny drones, small propellers (1 to 4 inches) with light plastic builds are good enough.
